# Organization
Water Engineers, Inc is a small Water Resource Engineering Consultant Firm based in California. They were established in 2020 and have been steadily growing in size. Their organizational mission is to inform sustainable water resource management through data-driven problem-solving. They specialize in planning, modeling, and analyzing the supply of surface water provided by Earth's hydrologic cycle to discrete geographical units called watersheds. Government stakeholders use this service to inform water resource management decisions at the state and local levels, directly affecting the general public.

As of today, the organization provides services across Southern California to both local municipalities and state government agencies such as the California Department of Water Resources; most of their contacts are provided by San Diego and Los Angeles Counties. Historically, small government contracts, lasting 6 months to a year, comprised most of their projects. However, with recent operational and reputational growth, they have been taking on contracts lasting upwards of 2 years.

# Stakeholders
Stakeholders are the individuals, groups, and organizations that interact with, influence, or are affected by the organization's business activities. Understanding the organization's stakeholders provides context for the business ecosystem in which it operates and establishes the parties whose interests shape business decisions, opportunities, and constraints.
For the purposes of this project, stakeholders are grouped according to their primary relationship with the organization.

## Internal Stakeholders
### Leadership

>**Includes:** _Owners, executive leadership, partners, and directors._<br>
>**Relationship:** _Leadership governs the organization by defining strategic direction, establishing business objectives, allocating resources, and overseeing organizational performance._<br>
>**Vested Interest:** _The long-term success of the organization through sustained profitability, growth, operational excellence, and a strong professional reputation._<br>
>**Stake:** _Organizational performance directly affects their financial return, professional reputation, strategic objectives, and long-term viability of the business._<br>
### Operations
>**Includes:** _Hydrologic engineers, hydraulic engineers, GIS analysts, environmental scientists, data analysts, project managers, technical managers, and other staff responsible for delivering consulting services._<br>
>**Relationship:** _Operations executes the organization's consulting services by producing engineering analyses, hydrologic models, technical reports, and other client deliverables._<br>
>**Vested Interest:** _Deliver technically accurate, efficient, and defensible engineering services that satisfy client needs while supporting successful project delivery._<br>
>**Stake:** _Successful operations generate organizational revenue, strengthen professional credibility, improve career opportunities, and contribute to job security._<br>
### Business Support
>**Includes:** _Human Resources, Finance, Accounting, Legal, Procurement, Marketing, Business Development, and Administrative staff._<br>
>**Relationship:** _Business Support enables the organization by providing the administrative, financial, legal, and organizational services required for Leadership and Operations to function effectively._<br>
>**Vested Interest:** _Maintain efficient internal business functions that support organizational stability, compliance, financial health, and employee success._<br>
>**Stake:** _The effectiveness of the organization directly influences employment stability, departmental performance, professional reputation, and organizational value._<br>
### Technology
>**Includes:** _Information Technology, software developers, database administrators, system administrators, cybersecurity personnel, and technical support._<br>
>**Relationship:** _Technology provides and maintains the information systems, software, databases, and infrastructure that support the organization's operations and business processes._<br>
>**Vested Interest:** _Provide secure, reliable, maintainable, and effective technology that enables efficient business operations._<br>
>**Stake:** _Reliable technology improves operational performance and organizational productivity, while poor technology can reduce confidence in the department, increase operational risk, and negatively affect organizational performance._<br>
 

## External Stakeholders
### Regulatory Agencies
>**Examples:** _California Department of Water Resources, San Diego County Water Authority, City of San Diego Public Utilities Department, and other federal, state, regional, or local agencies responsible for water resource management._<br>
>**Relationship:** _Regulatory agencies establish policies, regulations, permitting requirements, and public infrastructure that influence the engineering services performed by the organization. They may also engage the organization as consulting clients._<br>
>**Vested Interest:** _Receive technically sound, defensible engineering analyses that support informed regulatory and infrastructure decisions._<br>
>**Stake:** _Poor engineering analyses can contribute to ineffective regulatory decisions, legal challenges, reputational damage, and adverse impacts on public safety or environmental resources._<br>
### Public Organizations
>**Examples:** _Environmental advocacy groups, homeowner associations, watershed organizations, and other community groups._<br>
>**Relationship:** _Public organizations represent community and environmental interests that may be affected by engineering studies, infrastructure projects, or regulatory decisions supported by the organization's work._<br>
>**Vested Interest:** _Ensure that engineering decisions appropriately consider environmental, social, and community interests._<br>
>**Stake:** _Engineering recommendations and resulting regulatory decisions may positively or negatively affect the communities and interests they represent._<br>
### Private Organizations
>**Examples:** _Agricultural businesses, developers, utilities, industrial facilities, and other private enterprises affected by water resource management._<br>
>**Relationship:** _Private organizations operate within the regulatory environment influenced by the organization's engineering services and may also engage the organization directly as clients or project partners._<br>
>**Vested Interest:** _Receive engineering services and regulatory outcomes that support sustainable and economically viable business operations._<br>
>**Stake:** _Engineering analyses and subsequent regulatory decisions can directly influence operational costs, water availability, development opportunities, regulatory compliance, and overall business performance._<br>
### Data Providers
>**Examples:** _Government agencies, research institutions, universities, and organizations that publish environmental and geospatial datasets._<br>
>**Relationship:** _Data providers supply the environmental, hydrologic, climatic, and geospatial datasets that underpin the organization's technical analyses and modeling activities._<br>
>**Vested Interest:** _Ensure that their datasets are accessible, trusted, and effectively used to support scientific and engineering decision-making._<br>
>**Stake:** _The credibility, adoption, and continued support of their data products depend on users being able to rely on the quality and value of the information they provide._<br>
